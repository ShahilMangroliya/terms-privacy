What is Artist’s Grid Maker?

Artist’s Grid Maker is a tool for artists and drawing enthusiasts that helps you use photos as drawing references. The Application allows you to:

  • Select images from your camera or photo gallery using your device's native image picker
  • Apply artistic filters and pencil sketch effects to turn photos into drawing references
  • Overlay customizable grids over images for accurate proportions
  • Zoom and pan on images for detailed work
  • Open a distraction-free fullscreen view (free users unlock this by watching a short rewarded ad; premium users get it without ads)
  • Purchase a one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade to use the Application ad-free

Important: All image processing is done locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers or transmitted to third parties. The Application does not require or request camera or photo gallery permissions. Instead, it uses your device's system image picker, which allows you to select specific images you want to use without granting broad access to your entire photo library.


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?
User Provided Information

The Application does not require registration or login. You can use all features of the Application without providing any personal information. When you select images using your device's system picker, those images are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

If you choose to make an in-app purchase (for example, the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade), the purchase itself is processed by the Google Play Store (and, where applicable, the Apple App Store). The Service Provider does not see, collect, or store your payment card details, billing address, or Google/Apple account credentials. To validate the purchase across devices and reinstalls, an anonymous purchase identifier is shared with our purchase management provider, RevenueCat, which links the entitlement to a randomly generated app user ID.


Locally Stored Information

The Application stores a small amount of information directly on your device (using the operating system's standard storage) so that it can function correctly. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the Application. It includes:

  • A list of your most recently opened images (URIs only — the photos themselves are not copied) so the home screen can display recents
  • Your premium / ad-free entitlement status (cached so the app knows on launch whether to show ads)
  • UI preferences such as onboarding completion, the last time the upgrade screen was shown, daily-tip state, and similar app state

Automatically Collected Information

The Application may automatically collect limited, anonymized information through third-party services (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, RevenueCat) to improve functionality, deliver advertisements, and operate in-app purchases. This information may include:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage analytics (screens viewed, features used, including paywall and rewarded-ad interactions)
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Advertising identifiers for personalized ads (where you have consented and where required by law)
  • Anonymous purchase / entitlement state used to validate and restore in-app purchases

This data is anonymized and used solely for improving app performance, providing relevant advertisements, and maintaining your in-app purchase entitlements.

Advertising and Rewarded Ads

Free users may see App Open, Interstitial, and Native ads, and may choose to watch a Rewarded ad to unlock the fullscreen view. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional — declining to watch one only means that specific feature is not unlocked for that session. Ads are served by Google AdMob and may be personalized using advertising identifiers where you have provided consent and where personalized advertising is permitted by your device settings and local law (for example, ATT on iOS and the IAB TCF prompt where required in the EEA / UK).


In-App Purchases and Premium Status

The Application offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade. When you tap Continue on the upgrade screen, the Google Play Store (or, where applicable, the Apple App Store) handles the entire payment flow. The Service Provider does not receive your payment information. After a successful purchase, your entitlement is verified and stored anonymously by RevenueCat and cached locally on your device so the app can immediately disable ads on subsequent launches. You can restore a previously made purchase at any time from the Settings screen — this asks the store and RevenueCat to re-confirm your entitlement, which may briefly contact those services.

You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.
