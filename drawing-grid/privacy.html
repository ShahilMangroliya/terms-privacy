What is Drawing Grid?

Drawing Grid is a tool for artists and drawing enthusiasts that helps you use photos as drawing references. The Application allows you to:

  • Select images from your camera or photo gallery using your device's native image picker
  • Apply artistic filters and pencil sketch effects to turn photos into drawing references
  • Overlay customizable grids over images for accurate proportions
  • Zoom and pan on images for detailed work

Important: All image processing is done locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers or transmitted to third parties. The Application does not require or request camera or photo gallery permissions. Instead, it uses your device's system image picker, which allows you to select specific images you want to use without granting broad access to your entire photo library.
